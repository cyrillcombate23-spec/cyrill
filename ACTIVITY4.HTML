Starting My Journey

To start my day, I wake up at 6:30 AM if I have a 7:30 AM class—that’s my daily routine. I also get the child I look after ready, since the little one stays with me every morning. My mom irons my clothes the night before because she likes them perfectly pressed. I skip breakfast at home and head straight to school, where I eat instead; after that, I shower, commute, and go to class.

My College Experience

The course is tough, but I can handle it because I want to graduate. I’m my mom’s only child, and I want to give her a good life. College life is actually enjoyable, provided you find friends who will stick with you through the hard times and the work; having that kind of support gives you a better chance of graduating since you have someone with you every step of the way.

My Future Goals!

My goal is to graduate and provide a good life for my mom, since she is the only one I have; I want to repay her for all her hardships. Her only wish is for me to finish my studies and have a small shop of her own, so I will make that happen, no matter how difficult my studies may be.
